wire-codec · one declaration, three wire formats

Four ways to say the same value, and the gate that keeps them from lying

The wire format is usually the decision a game cannot revisit: the client shipped, the protocol is in the field, and changing it is a coordinated release. Here it is configuration - the four codecs are held to one canonical resolution, and the sizes above are the same Move value in each of them. The gate is the part that makes it a claim rather than a claim about a claim: 21 checks across every declared message and every codec, and the four faults it refuses instead of decoding into something plausible.
